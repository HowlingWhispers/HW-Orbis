# Community and Multiplayer Rules

Status: community policy draft
Applies to: Fabula multiplayer and Discord-linked community features
Last updated: 6 September 2026

## Plain-language summary

Private roleplay is private. Multiplayer is shared space.

Joining Fabula multiplayer means respecting other users, their boundaries, their privacy and the rules of the world you enter.

## 1. Eligibility

Fabula multiplayer may require:

- a registered account;
- active membership in The Howling Whispers Discord server; and
- any active multiplayer entitlement or subscription required at the time.

Private use of eligible shared content may remain available without multiplayer eligibility.

## 2. Presence is not consent

Seeing another user in the same Fabula location does not mean they have agreed to interact with you.

Users must use the available interaction or shared-scene systems rather than treating location presence as automatic consent.

## 3. Private scenes stay private

Users must not attempt to obtain, expose, scrape, intercept or redistribute another user's private AI conversation, private memory, hidden context, branches or unrelated session history.

## 4. Shared scenes

A shared scene is limited to the participants who intentionally joined it.

Users who happen to occupy the same world or location are not automatically participants.

## 5. Harassment and stalking

Do not use multiplayer presence, Discord identity, world location, block evasion or repeated scene requests to stalk, threaten or harass another user.

## 6. World rules

Each world may have its own authored rules, permissions and canon restrictions.

Entering a world means accepting those world-specific participation rules in addition to the platform rules.

## 7. Adult and sensitive areas

Some worlds or locations may be restricted by age, account status, role, world rule or other eligibility requirement.

Access to an adult or sensitive location does not waive privacy or consent requirements.

## 8. Moderation

Community or multiplayer access may be warned, restricted, suspended or removed for serious or repeated violations.

Where practical, users should be told the reason and have an appeal route.

Immediate action may be taken for threats, severe harassment, security abuse, unlawful material, evidence preservation or serious safety concerns.

## 9. Discord relationship

Discord membership is a community-access condition where specified. Discord also has its own terms and platform rules.

Losing Discord-server membership may remove multiplayer eligibility without necessarily removing ordinary private roleplay access.

## 10. Reports

Users should report serious multiplayer abuse through the service's moderation or report tools rather than attempting retaliation or public doxxing.
