# Illegal Content, Reports and Legal Requests Policy

Status: legal and operational draft
Applies to: The Howling Whispers services, including Orbis, Speculus and Fabula
Last updated: 6 September 2026

## Plain-language summary

We do not promise to ignore unlawful content, valid court orders or serious abuse.

Users remain responsible for their own conduct. The Howling Whispers may remove or restrict content, preserve relevant records and respond to binding legal process where required.

## 1. Reporting allegedly illegal content

The production service should provide an easy-to-find reporting route for allegedly illegal content.

A useful report should include, where available:

- the exact content or location being reported;
- a URL, asset ID, message ID or other precise identifier;
- why the reporter believes the content is illegal;
- the law or right involved if known;
- supporting evidence where appropriate; and
- contact information for follow-up unless anonymous reporting is legally permitted and sufficient.

Reports should be handled in good faith and as efficiently as reasonably possible.

## 2. Review and action

After receiving a sufficiently specific report, The Howling Whispers may:

- leave the content available;
- restrict visibility while reviewing it;
- remove or disable access;
- restrict the responsible account;
- preserve relevant records;
- request more information from the reporter;
- notify the affected user where appropriate; or
- refer the matter to a competent authority where legally required.

The service should distinguish between content that violates internal rules and content that is alleged to be illegal.

## 3. No automatic guilt

A report is an allegation, not automatic proof of wrongdoing.

Where practical and lawful, affected users should have a reasonable opportunity to understand and challenge moderation decisions.

This does not prevent immediate restriction where delay could create serious harm, evidence loss or legal risk.

## 4. Evidence preservation

The Howling Whispers may preserve relevant account, content, moderation, security and technical records when reasonably necessary to:

- investigate abuse;
- respond to an appeal;
- comply with a preservation request or legal order;
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
- investigate account compromise or fraud; or
- protect users or infrastructure.

Preservation does not mean the content remains publicly accessible.

## 5. Law-enforcement and court requests

The Howling Whispers may respond to valid and binding legal requests, including orders from courts, competent regulators and law-enforcement authorities.

We may require the requesting authority to use the proper legal process and provide enough information to identify the account or records sought.

We do not promise to provide information merely because someone claiming to be an authority asks informally.

## 6. Jurisdiction

The service may be affected by more than one jurisdiction.

Relevant law may include:

- law applicable to the service operator;
- German law where infrastructure or hosting is located or German law otherwise applies;
- European Union law;
- law applicable to the user's own location or conduct; and
- law of another competent jurisdiction where a valid legal order has effect.

The physical location of a server is not the sole test for every legal question.

Users are responsible for their own local legal obligations.

## 7. Emergency situations

Where applicable law allows it, The Howling Whispers may disclose limited information or take emergency protective action when reasonably necessary to address an imminent threat of death or serious physical harm.

Emergency disclosure should be limited to what is reasonably necessary under the circumstances.

## 8. Account compromise

If activity appears to result from account theft, malware or another compromise, the service may temporarily restrict the account, invalidate sessions or preserve security records while ownership is verified.

A compromised account does not automatically prove that the legitimate owner intentionally performed every action recorded on it.

## 9. AI-generated content

AI output may be unexpected.

The appearance of unlawful or potentially unlawful material in an AI response does not by itself prove that the user requested or intended it.

Investigations should consider available context, including prompts, repeated conduct, user actions after generation and whether the material was stored, shared or intentionally continued.

## 10. User responsibility

Users remain responsible for intentional unlawful prompts, uploads, distribution, publication, harassment, fraud, exploitation, infringement and other misuse attributable to them.

The Howling Whispers does not authorize unlawful conduct by providing general-purpose fictional characters, worlds, roleplay systems or model connections.

## 11. Transparency and privacy

Legal and moderation disclosures should be limited to what is appropriate for the request and applicable law.

The service should avoid unnecessarily exposing private roleplay or unrelated user information.

## 12. Contact point

Before public launch, the service should publish a dedicated contact point such as:

```text
Illegal-content reports: [REPORT EMAIL OR FORM]
Legal process: [LEGAL CONTACT EMAIL]
Security emergencies: [SECURITY CONTACT EMAIL]
```

This document is an operational/legal draft and must be completed with real contact details before launch.
