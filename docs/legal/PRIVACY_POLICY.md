# Privacy Policy

Status: legal draft for review
Applies to: The Howling Whispers services, including Orbis/Coda, Speculus and Fabula
Last updated: 6 September 2026

## Plain-language summary

We only want to collect the data we actually need to run the service.

Guest Speculus sessions are intended to be temporary and non-persistent. Registered features may store account data, settings, authored content, saved sessions and other information needed to provide the service. Fabula multiplayer may also need presence and moderation data.

We do not promise anonymity where account, security, billing, moderation or legal records necessarily exist.

The operator of The Howling Whispers is the controller for personal data processed directly by the service.

## 2. Data we may process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

- Discord or other identity-provider account identifiers;
- display name, avatar and profile information made available through the login provider;
- account settings and preferences;
- authored characters, worlds, personas, locations, memories and related library records;
- saved roleplay sessions and memory where the user has a persistent account feature;
- Fabula presence, location and shared-scene participation data;
- payment status and transaction references from a payment provider;
- security information such as IP address, timestamps, session identifiers and abuse-prevention records;
- moderation reports, appeals and enforcement history;
- support correspondence; and
- technical logs needed to diagnose failures and protect the service.

We should avoid storing payment-card details ourselves where a payment processor can handle them.

## 3. Guest Speculus sessions

Guest Speculus use is designed to be ephemeral.

Guest roleplay history, persistent character memory, persona progress and session state should not be written into permanent account storage merely because a guest used the service.

Temporary processing in memory, short-lived caches or transport logs may still occur where technically necessary to deliver the session, secure the service or diagnose failures.

## 4. Why we process data

We may process personal data to:

- provide login, account and library features;
- provide saved sessions and persistent memory where enabled;
- provide Fabula multiplayer presence and shared scenes;
- verify Discord-server membership for community-gated multiplayer access;
- process or verify purchases and subscriptions;
- prevent fraud, abuse, account compromise and attacks;
- enforce the Terms of Service and community rules;
- respond to support requests;
- comply with legal obligations and valid legal process; and
- improve reliability and diagnose technical problems.

## 5. Legal bases

Depending on the processing activity, the legal basis may include:

- performance of a contract with the user;
- compliance with a legal obligation;
- legitimate interests such as service security, abuse prevention and defending legal claims;
- consent, where the law requires consent; and
- other lawful bases available under applicable data-protection law.

The correct legal basis should be documented per processing activity before production launch.

## 6. AI providers and user-selected endpoints

When a user selects a third-party AI provider, prompts and relevant context may be sent to that provider in order to generate a response.

The provider may process that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

If a user configures a personal local model or a remote endpoint, the user is responsible for understanding who operates that endpoint, what it logs and where the data goes.

The Howling Whispers cannot guarantee the privacy practices of an endpoint that the user supplied and that we do not operate.

See `AI_PROVIDERS_AND_MODEL_POLICY.md` for more detail.

## 7. Discord

Where Discord login or membership verification is used, Discord may independently process information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Signing in with Discord is separate from joining The Howling Whispers Discord server.

The service should request only the Discord permissions reasonably required for the feature being provided.

## 8. Payments

Where paid Fabula multiplayer or another paid feature is offered, payment processing should normally be handled by a dedicated payment provider.

We may receive information such as:

- payment status;
- transaction or subscription identifier;
- plan or entitlement;
- billing country or tax information where required; and
- limited customer information needed to administer the purchase.

The payment provider's own privacy policy also applies to information it processes.

## 9. Data sharing

We may share information only where reasonably necessary with categories such as:

- hosting and infrastructure providers;
- authentication providers;
- AI providers selected for generation;
- payment processors;
- email or support providers;
- security and anti-abuse providers;
- professional advisers; and
- public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

We do not treat a user's private roleplay as public merely because that user is in a multiplayer location.

## 10. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Germany or the European Economic Area.

Where required, appropriate transfer mechanisms and safeguards should be used.

A user's own choice of external or local endpoint may also determine where their data is processed.

## 11. Retention

We should keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal, security, billing and dispute requirements.

Examples:

- guest session content: temporary by design;
- account data: while the account is active and for a reasonable deletion/backup period afterward;
- billing records: for the legally required accounting/tax period;
- security logs: for a limited period appropriate to detecting abuse and investigating incidents;
- moderation/evidence records: as long as reasonably necessary for enforcement, appeals, safety or legal claims.

A separate internal retention schedule should define exact periods.

## 12. User rights

Where applicable, users may have rights to:

- access personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request deletion;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive portable data where the law provides that right;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- complain to a competent data-protection authority.

Some information may need to be retained despite a deletion request where the law allows or requires it, for example billing records, fraud evidence or records needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data and account access.

No online service can promise perfect security.

Users are responsible for protecting their own Discord, email, API keys, local endpoints and account credentials.

## 14. Children and account eligibility

The service may contain fictional minor characters for legitimate narrative purposes. That does not mean the service is intended for unsupervised use by children.

Age requirements for account creation, adult content and paid services must be shown clearly in the applicable interface and must follow the law that applies to the user and the service.

## 15. Legal requests and emergencies

We may preserve or disclose information where required by binding legal process or where applicable law permits disclosure to address a serious and imminent threat to life or safety.

We will not promise that user data can never be disclosed to authorities.

## 16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes.

Material changes should be communicated in a reasonable and visible way where required.
