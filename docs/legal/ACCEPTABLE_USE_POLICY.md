# Acceptable Use Policy

Status: legal and community policy draft
Applies to: The Howling Whispers services, including Orbis, Speculus and Fabula
Last updated: 6 September 2026

## Plain-language rule

The Howling Whispers gives users creative freedom, but it does not give anyone permission to break the law, harm other people, invade privacy, steal content, attack systems or misuse the service.

Users are responsible for their own prompts, uploads, roleplay choices, publications and conduct.

## 1. Fictional freedom does not override law

The service may allow broad fictional roleplay, including dark, violent, disturbing, sexual, historical or otherwise sensitive themes where the selected provider and feature allow them.

The existence of a fictional character, world, tool or AI model does not mean that every possible use is lawful.

Users must comply with the laws that apply to them and with any mandatory law that applies to the service.

## 2. Fictional minor characters

The service may contain fictional characters who are minors for legitimate narrative purposes such as:

- family relationships;
- childhood and development;
- genealogy;
- schools, guardianship and upbringing;
- historical events;
- world simulation; and
- character backstory.

The presence of a fictional minor character is not authorization, encouragement or approval of unlawful sexual material or exploitation.

Users are responsible for ensuring that any roleplay involving fictional minors complies with applicable law.

If a user's jurisdiction prohibits a category of generated, requested, stored or distributed content, the user must not use the service to create, obtain, keep or distribute that content.

## 3. Unexpected AI output

AI output can be unpredictable.

A model may occasionally generate explicit, violent, disturbing, offensive, unlawful-looking or otherwise unwanted content that the user did not request.

Unexpected output does not automatically mean that the user intended or endorsed it.

However, users must not intentionally continue, preserve, publish, distribute or exploit unlawful material once they are aware of it.

## 4. Prohibited misuse

Users must not intentionally use the service to:

- commit or facilitate fraud, theft, extortion or other crime;
- threaten, stalk, harass or unlawfully target another person;
- publish another person's private information without lawful authority;
- impersonate another person for deceptive or harmful purposes;
- distribute malware, credential stealers, spyware or destructive code;
- attack, overload, scan or bypass service security without permission;
- obtain another user's private roleplay, account data or hidden session state without authorization;
- evade bans, access restrictions or eligibility checks;
- upload or distribute content they do not have the legal right to use;
- use the service to distribute unlawful sexual abuse or exploitation material;
- use the service to facilitate trafficking, coercion or exploitation;
- interfere with investigations, evidence preservation or lawful moderation; or
- use the service in a manner that is otherwise unlawful under applicable law.

## 5. Roleplay involving real people

Users must take extra care when using real people's names, likenesses or personal information.

The service must not be used to unlawfully impersonate, defame, harass, exploit or invade the privacy of a real person.

## 6. Intellectual property

Users must have the rights or permission needed for content they upload, publish or distribute.

A user remains responsible for claims arising from their own unauthorized use of copyrighted works, trademarks, characters, images, recordings or other protected material.

See `CONTENT_AND_IP_POLICY.md`.

## 7. Multiplayer conduct

Fabula multiplayer is a shared community space.

Users must not:

- force another user into a shared scene without the required consent or participation flow;
- attempt to expose another user's private AI conversation;
- use presence information to stalk or harass another user;
- abuse multiplayer to evade blocks, moderation or community restrictions; or
- intentionally disrupt shared world infrastructure.

Private roleplay and shared multiplayer scenes are separate by design.

## 8. Provider rules still apply

Users must also follow the applicable terms, content rules and technical restrictions of the selected AI provider, payment provider, identity provider or user-supplied endpoint.

A provider refusing a request does not create a right to bypass that provider's restrictions through The Howling Whispers.

A personal local model may have fewer provider-side restrictions, but the user still remains responsible for applicable law and the model's license.

## 9. Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may:

- warn the user;
- restrict a feature;
- remove or disable access to content;
- suspend multiplayer access;
- suspend or terminate an account;
- preserve relevant records;
- block abusive traffic; or
- make disclosures where legally required.

We may act immediately where reasonably necessary to protect users, infrastructure, evidence or legal compliance.

## 10. No transfer of responsibility

A user's unlawful act does not become the responsibility of The Howling Whispers merely because the service, a fictional character, an AI integration or server infrastructure was used as a tool.

At the same time, nothing in this policy attempts to exclude responsibility that the law itself places on The Howling Whispers.

## 11. Reporting

Users should be provided with a clear way to report illegal content, account compromise, abuse or serious safety concerns.

The production service should publish the relevant contact or reporting form before launch.
