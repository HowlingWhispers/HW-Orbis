# Payments, Refunds and Cancellation Policy

Status: legal draft for review
Applies to: paid The Howling Whispers features, including any paid Fabula multiplayer entitlement
Last updated: 6 September 2026

## Plain-language summary

If a paid feature exists, the price, billing period and renewal terms must be shown clearly before purchase.

We may generally treat used service periods as non-refundable, but only to the extent permitted by applicable consumer law. Mandatory withdrawal, refund, chargeback and consumer rights are not waived by this policy.

## 1. Prices

Prices must be shown before purchase and should state whether tax is included where applicable.

The service should clearly show:

- the feature being purchased;
- the total price;
- the billing period;
- whether the purchase renews automatically;
- how to cancel renewal; and
- any eligibility requirements, such as Discord-server membership for Fabula multiplayer.

## 2. Fabula multiplayer eligibility

Payment alone does not necessarily make a user eligible for Fabula multiplayer.

Where the service requires Discord-server membership, a registered account and an active multiplayer entitlement, all applicable requirements must be satisfied.

If a user knowingly purchases an entitlement while not meeting a clearly disclosed eligibility requirement, access may remain unavailable until the requirement is met.

Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

## 3. Recurring subscriptions

If a subscription renews automatically, the renewal terms and billing interval must be disclosed before purchase.

Users must be given a reasonable way to cancel future renewal.

Cancellation normally stops future renewals and does not erase access already paid for during the current billing period unless the service states otherwise or the law requires a different result.

## 4. Refunds

Except where required by law or expressly promised by The Howling Whispers, payments for a service period that has already been provided are generally non-refundable.

This is not a waiver of mandatory consumer rights.

Refunds may still be appropriate where, for example:

- the law requires one;
- the same charge was duplicated;
- a technical failure prevented meaningful access for a material part of the paid period;
- a billing error occurred;
- the service explicitly promised a refund; or
- another legally valid reason applies.

## 5. EU/EEA withdrawal rights

Consumers in the European Union or European Economic Area may have a statutory withdrawal period for distance contracts.

The exact rules depend on whether the purchase is legally treated as a service, digital content or another contract type.

If immediate performance is requested during a statutory withdrawal period, the checkout flow should obtain any express request, consent and acknowledgement required by law.

Where legally permitted, a consumer who withdraws after requesting immediate performance may be responsible for the proportionate value of service already supplied.

The service must not rely on a blanket 'no refunds' statement to remove rights that cannot legally be waived.

## 6. Immediate activation

If paid multiplayer is activated immediately after purchase, the checkout should clearly explain what immediate activation means for withdrawal rights before payment is completed.

Required consent must not be hidden in small print or a pre-ticked box.

## 7. Failed payments and chargebacks

If payment fails, expires, is reversed or is charged back, the corresponding paid entitlement may be suspended while the billing state is resolved.

Fraudulent chargebacks or payment abuse may result in account restrictions.

A legitimate chargeback or statutory consumer complaint must not be treated as misconduct merely because the user exercised a legal right.

## 8. Loss of Discord membership

Where Discord membership is a disclosed condition of Fabula multiplayer, losing that membership may make the user ineligible to enter multiplayer spaces.

This does not automatically decide whether a refund is legally required. Refund rights depend on the circumstances, the reason for the loss of eligibility, the contract terms and applicable law.

## 9. Service discontinuation

If The Howling Whispers permanently discontinues a prepaid paid feature before the end of a paid period, it should provide an appropriate remedy where required, which may include continued access for the remaining period, a replacement service, account credit or a proportionate refund.

## 10. Price changes

New prices should apply prospectively.

For recurring subscriptions, users should receive reasonable notice of material price changes where required and should be able to cancel future renewal before the changed price takes effect.

## 11. Payment provider

Payments may be processed by a third-party payment provider.

That provider's own terms, privacy policy and payment rules may also apply.

The Howling Whispers should avoid storing full payment-card credentials itself where a payment provider can handle them securely.
